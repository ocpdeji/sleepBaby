## 5. Social Media Strategy (Where to Post)

### Top 3 Platforms (in order of priority):

**1. TikTok** — Your #1 organic discovery engine
- Post 4–6x per week
- Hook in first 2 seconds: "Nobody talks about Night 3..." / "The mistake I made with all 6 of my babies..."
- Format: Talking-head face-to-camera, stitch trending sleep advice, debunk myths
- Always end with: "Free baby sleep cheat sheet — link in bio"

**2. Pinterest** — Slow burn, but converts at 5x the rate of Instagram
- Pin 5–10x per day (use Tailwind for scheduling)
- Create boards: "Baby Sleep 0–6 Months", "Twin Sleep Tips", "Breastfeeding & Sleep", "Toddler Bedtime"
- Each pin: vertical infographic graphic with a hook + link to a blog post that has your opt-in form

**3. Instagram Reels** — Repurpose TikToks
- Cross-post every TikTok as a Reel within 24 hours
- Stories: behind-the-scenes "mom of 6" content
- Save your best Reels as Highlights: "Sleep Tips", "Twin Mom", "Night Wakings"

### Skip (for now):
- **Facebook** — Older audience, low engagement for this niche
- **Twitter/X** — Wrong format
- **LinkedIn** — Wrong audience
- **YouTube** — High effort, save for year 2

## 9. Pricing Psychology

### Don't go below $19. Don't start above $39.

- **$9** — Devalues the work. People don't trust "cheap" for advice.
- **$19** — Launch price. Feels like an impulse buy. Drives volume.
- **$29** — Standard retail. Implies premium quality.
- **$39–49** — Premium bundle (eBook + planners). For the "I want EVERYTHING" buyer.
- **$99+** — Save for a future course or 1:1 sleep consult upsell.

### The launch discount is essential.
Always launch at $19, raise to $29 after 7 days. Add a public note: *"Founding price closed. Thank you to our early community."* This creates loyalty AND social proof.

## 10. Refund Policy & Customer Service

**Policy:** 30-day full refund, no questions asked.

This sounds risky. It's not. Why:
- Refund rate for digital books in this niche is typically 2–4%
- A "no questions asked" policy reduces purchase friction (more sales)
- Confident refund policies signal you stand behind the product
- Most refunders never finish the book — your real risk is **bad reviews from unfinished readers**

**Auto-reply for refund requests:**
> "Of course. I've processed your refund — it will appear in 5–7 business days. If you have a moment, I'd love to know what didn't work for you so the next edition is better. Either way, thank you for trying it. Sleep is coming. — Six & Thriving"
