## ENGAGEMENT TIPS (read these once)

1. **Reply to every comment in the first 24 hours.** Even just an emoji. The algorithm rewards conversation.
2. **Ask one question at the end of every post.** "What's your baby's sleep personality?" gets engagement.
3. **Pin your highest-performing post to the top of your profile** — it's your free billboard.
4. **Use trending sounds on TikTok** — even on talking-head content. The algorithm prioritizes posts using rising sounds.
5. **Cross-post within 24 hours, not 24 minutes.** Same content same day on multiple platforms gets penalized.
6. **Don't add the link to the caption.** Says "link in bio." Always.
7. **Repurpose ruthlessly.** A 30-second TikTok = a Reel + a Pinterest pin + a tweet + an email subject line.
