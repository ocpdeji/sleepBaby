## SECTION 10: FAQ

### Headline:
## Questions other parents have asked.

### Q&A format (click to expand each):

**Q: What age is this for?**
A: Birth to 24 months for active sleep work. The newborn chapter (Ch.15) is for under 8 weeks. Chapter 11 covers toddler sleep through age 3.

**Q: I'm breastfeeding — is this for me?**
A: Yes. Chapter 13 is dedicated specifically to breastfeeding moms. Independent sleep and breastfeeding are absolutely compatible. Your supply will not disappear. Your bond will not break.

**Q: I tried sleep training before and it failed. What now?**
A: Chapter 7 contains The Reset Protocol — a 5-step diagnostic for parents who've already tried. The book directly addresses why most attempts fail and how to fix it.

**Q: Is this just cry-it-out repackaged?**
A: No. Extinction (CIO) is one of five methods covered. The book also teaches Ferber, Chair Method, Fading, and Pick-Up/Put-Down. You choose what fits your family. The book never tells you to ignore a distressed baby.

**Q: I have twins. Is there real twin content here?**
A: Yes. Chapter 14 is specifically for twins and includes the synchronization principles I used with my own. Plus the Twins Sync Planner is included in the Premium Bundle.

**Q: Is this safe for my baby?**
A: Yes. Chapter 5 is built around AAP safe-sleep guidelines and the bonus Safe Sleep Checklist runs through every nightly safety check. Always consult your pediatrician for your baby's specific medical situation.

**Q: How long until I see results?**
A: Most families see meaningful change within 7 nights. Some take 10–14. The book is built to set realistic week-1 expectations and prevent quitting on Night 3 (the hardest).

**Q: I'm a single parent. Will this work?**
A: Yes. The Partner Briefing chapter addresses single-parent strategies directly. You're doing something extraordinary, and the system is designed to be implementable solo.

**Q: What if my baby has a medical condition?**
A: Always consult your pediatrician first. The book is designed for healthy babies whose paediatrician has cleared them for sleep training. NICU and premature babies are addressed specifically in Chapter 14.

**Q: What format is the book?**
A: PDF. Instant download after purchase. Read on any device — phone, tablet, computer, or print it.

## TECHNICAL NOTES FOR YOUR DEVELOPER / WEBSITE BUILDER

### Page structure recommendations:

1. **Sticky CTA bar** at top: "Get the book — $19 (48hr launch price) → Buy"
2. **Above the fold:** Hero + primary CTA. Should pass the "5-second test" — can a stranger tell what the product is and click buy in 5 seconds?
3. **Mobile first:** 70%+ of buyers will be on phone, often at 2am. Test EVERY section on mobile.
4. **Page load speed:** Compress images. Aim for <3 second load time on 4G.
5. **Stripe / Gumroad / Payhip** for checkout. Avoid sending people to a separate cart.
6. **Exit-intent popup:** When someone tries to leave, popup with "Wait — get the free Cheat Sheet first." Captures emails of non-buyers.
7. **Schema markup:** Add Book schema for SEO (Google can show price + reviews in search results).
8. **Pixel tracking:** Install Meta Pixel + Google Analytics from day 1. You'll need this data for ads later.
