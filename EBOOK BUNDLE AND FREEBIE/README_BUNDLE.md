## ⚠️ Important Reminders

- **Always carry the disclaimer** ("Educational purposes only. Not medical advice.") on every product
- **Don't fake testimonials.** Wait for real ones. Readers can smell fake.
- **Don't promise results.** Promise a plan and a refund.
- **Reply to every customer email** in the first 90 days. This is how you build superfans.
- **Track your numbers.** Open rate, click rate, conversion rate, refund rate. Without data, you can't improve.
