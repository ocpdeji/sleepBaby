You’ve got questions

I’ve got answers.

Is your approach gentle?
Yes. Every plan is responsive, attachment-aware, and built around your child’s temperament and your family values. No one-size-fits-all scripts.
Will this work for our situation?
Most families see improvement when they have a clear plan and steady support. We’ll look at age, schedule, feeding, temperament, routines, and your comfort level.
How soon will we see results?
Many parents notice calmer bedtimes within days. More established sleep patterns usually build over one to three weeks depending on your starting point.
What ages do you support?
I support families with babies, toddlers, and preschoolers, with age-appropriate strategies for newborn foundations through big-kid bedtime battles.
Do you offer one-on-one support?
Yes. Private support packages include a custom sleep plan, check-ins, and compassionate troubleshooting so you never feel alone.
